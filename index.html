Privacy and controls

  • The app backend listens only on this computer, not on your network.
  • Only text files from a folder you choose are read. They are sent only to the local model runtime.
  • The app does not record the screen, keystrokes, or activity in other apps.
  • Files are never changed automatically. Save a draft only when you choose.
  • Chat history and activity are stored in this browser’s local storage. Use Clear history to remove them.
